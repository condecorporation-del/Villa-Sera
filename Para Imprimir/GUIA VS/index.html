Main living room — Fire TV Stick (channels & apps)
1
Turn on the TCL and select Fire TV HDMI
With the TV remote, power on and select the HDMI input where the Fire TV Stick is plugged in (usually “Fire TV” or HDMI1). If you see antenna/cable, switch sources until the Fire menu appears.
2
Use the Fire TV remote
The small black remote controls the Stick. The Home button always returns to the main menu. If unresponsive, check batteries or aim at the TV.
3
Watch live TV
On the home screen, scroll to “Your apps & channels” or the apps row. Open the live TV / channel list (already set up). Wait for the guide to load; the first time may take a few seconds.
4
Browse channels
Use the arrow keys to choose a channel or category; press OK to watch. Back (←) goes up one level. If the picture freezes, hold Home and close the app, or unplug the Fire TV for 10 s and reconnect.
5
Netflix, Prime, YouTube…
Open them from Fire TV home or with the remote mic: “Alexa, open Netflix” (if the remote has a microphone). Use your own account where required.
Other rooms (TCL smart): Open Netflix, YouTube, Prime, etc. from the TV remote. On the same VillaSera_Guest, many TCL TVs support Chromecast or screen mirroring from your phone.
Sonos audio: Sonos app or direct AirPlay from Spotify/Apple Music. Speakers on terrace, living room, master, and kitchen — see page 07.
WiFi issues? Restart the router (location above). If it persists, contact Marlon.
Streaming accounts: Sign in to Netflix, Disney+, Prime, or others on each TV. Remember to sign out at check-out.

07 · Gym & beach
Treadmill — use & care
Villa Sera's treadmill faces the Sea of Cortés directly — always cover the belt after use.
Protective cover — required: Sea salt damages the belt and rails. Place the waterproof cover immediately after every use. Spray bottle with water and mild soap on the shelf — spray belt and rails, wipe, and cover.
Before use
1
Remove the cover
Lift the protective cover and fold it. Set it aside or on the equipment shelf.
2
Power on at the console
Press the power button on the main console. The screen lights up in 2–3 seconds.
3
Attach the safety clip
Clip the red emergency stop to your clothing. If you fall, it stops the belt automatically.
4
Use the controls
Start/Stop to begin and stop · ▲▼ for speed · Incline for grade. Buttons are labeled on the console.
After use — required
1
Power off
Press Stop then the power button. Wait until the belt stops completely.
2
Apply the spray
Water and mild soap solution in the shelf spray bottle. Spray belt and rails to remove sweat and salt.
3
Wipe with the cloth
Wipe the belt and rails. Let air dry for 1 minute.
4
Replace the cover
Always cover the treadmill when finished. Sea salt penetrates even when invisible and damages the belt over time.

07 · Grill
How to use the charcoal grill
Weber grill with chimney starter
On the main terrace. Charcoal, chimney starter, tongs, and lighter in the pool kitchen (Kitchen 2). No liquid fuel.

Lighting the grill — 7 steps
1
Fill chimney ¾ with charcoal
Charcoal is in the pool kitchen. Do not overfill.
2
Place starters underneath
1–2 natural wax blocks under the chimney grate. Never use liquid fuel.
3
Set chimney on the grill
All vents open for good airflow.
4
Light the starters
Use the long lighter through the bottom holes. No need to reach inside.
5
Wait 15–20 minutes
Ready when top coals glow orange with gray-white ash. Do not rush this step.
6
Pour charcoal and spread
Use heat gloves. Tilt the chimney to pour. Spread evenly with tongs. Place the grate on top.
7
Preheat 5 min → cook!
Brush the grate while hot. Ready to cook.
Safety: Never use liquid fuel. Close vents to extinguish. Wait 2 hrs before touching ash. Fire extinguisher: kitchen, lower left cabinet.
Cleanup after grilling
Brush the grate while still hot — cold grease is much harder to clean
Close all vents to extinguish the coals
Wait until fully cool (1–2 hrs) before moving ash
Dispose of cold ash in the metal bin by the grill — never in plastic bags
