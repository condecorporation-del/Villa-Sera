Laundry

LG washer and dryer tower. Follow these steps to use it correctly during your stay.

Step 1 · Power On Step 2 · Washer Step 3 · Dryer
⚠️

Use HE (High Efficiency) detergent only. Regular detergent can damage the washer. You'll find it on the upper shelf in the laundry room.

Visual reference
3-step overview
1

Power On

How to turn on the LG Tower

👆

Press and hold the power button for 2 to 3 seconds, until the control panel lights up. The tower will display the main screen, ready to use.

The power button is located on the central panel of the tower, between the washer (bottom) and the dryer (top).

2

Washer — Setup & Use

The washer is the bottom unit of the tower

A
Load laundry

Open the washer door (bottom unit) and load your laundry. Do not overfill — load to ¾ capacity for best results. Overloading prevents proper spinning and leaves clothes damp.

B
Add HE detergent

Pull out the detergent drawer (upper left of the washer). Pour HE detergent into the main compartment (the largest one). The recommended fill line is marked inside the drawer. Push the drawer closed. Do not exceed the recommended amount — excess detergent creates too much foam.

C
Select a cycle

Turn the dial to select the right cycle:

  • Normal — for everyday clothing (t-shirts, pants, casual wear)
  • Delicate — for fine fabrics, lingerie, or sensitive materials
  • Bedding — for sheets, pillowcases, and towels
  • Colors — for colored garments that may bleed

D
Start the washer

Press the Start / Pause (▶‖) button to begin the cycle. The door will lock automatically during the wash for safety. Do not force it open while the machine is running.

Typical duration: 45–60 minutes on a Normal cycle. The display will show the remaining time.

3

Dryer — Setup & Use

The dryer is the top unit of the tower

A
Clean the lint filter — required before every use

Open the dryer door (top unit). The lint filter is located inside the door. Pull it out, remove the accumulated lint by hand or with a cloth, and reinsert it fully.

A clogged filter can cause overheating and automatic shut-off. Cleaning it is the single most important step before using the dryer.

B
Select a cycle

Turn the dryer dial to choose the right cycle:

  • Normal / Cotton — for everyday cotton laundry
  • Bedding — for sheets, pillowcases, and comforters
  • Delicate / Low heat — for fine or synthetic fabrics
  • Quick Dry (30 min) — for lightly damp garments

C
Start the dryer

Transfer laundry directly from the washer. Do not leave wet clothes sitting for more than 30 minutes or they may develop an odor.

Press Start / Pause (▶‖) to begin. The display will show the remaining time.

Typical duration: 50–70 minutes depending on the cycle and load size.

Need help?

Cecilia can handle laundry for you. Please give advance notice to coordinate the service during your stay.

Towels & bedding

To request fresh towels or sheets without doing laundry yourself, leave them on the bathroom floor or contact the Villa Sera team.

Detergent available

HE detergent is available on the upper shelf in the laundry room. If it runs out, contact Marlon to have it restocked.

Delicate items

For very delicate garments (silk, wool, embroidered pieces), we recommend hand-washing in the sink in the service room.
